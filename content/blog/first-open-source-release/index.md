Shipping MolSim v0.1 as an open-source project taught us things that years of internal development hadn't.

## Documentation Is the Product

Nobody uses software they can't understand. We spent as much time on the README and examples as we did on the last round of performance optimizations.

## Community Takes Intentional Investment

Good intentions aren't enough. You have to actively respond to issues, welcome contributions, and make it easy for newcomers to get started.
